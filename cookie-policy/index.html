This policy explains how Daily Shot Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

Cookies and similar technologies on this website

Cookies are small files that a website can store on your device. Websites can also use technologies such as browser storage or tracking pixels to store information or read it from your device.

The pages served by Daily Shot do not set cookies or use browser storage, analytics tags, advertising trackers or third-party embeds. There are no cookie preferences to set on these pages.

Hosting and security

GitHub Pages hosts this site and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is server-side logging. See our privacy notice for how personal information is handled.

Google Maps and WhatsApp

Our directions and messaging links open Google Maps or WhatsApp when you choose to use them. These services may use cookies or similar technologies after you open them. You can read about their practices in the Google cookie policy and WhatsApp cookie policy.
