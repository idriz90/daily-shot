Privacy notice

This notice explains how Daily Shot Ltd handles personal information when you visit this website, contact us or enquire about private venue hire.

Who we are

Daily Shot Ltd is the controller for the personal information we use for these purposes. We are registered in England and Wales under company number 13866459. Our registered office is 22 Aldensley Road, London W6 0DH. Email info@dailyshotwarehouse.co.uk for privacy questions, requests or complaints.

What we use and why

  • General enquiries. When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we use the contact details and information you share to reply and follow up. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running the café.
  • Private venue hire. We use your contact details, correspondence and event details, such as the date and guest numbers, to prepare a quote or arrange a booking. If you are making a booking in your own name, our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract or performing that contract. If you contact us on behalf of someone else, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in arranging and managing the booking. We need enough information to reply, check availability and confirm the booking. Without it, we may be unable to help.
  • Website hosting and security. GitHub Pages receives your IP address when it serves the website and logs it for security. Our lawful basis for using hosting that processes this information is our legitimate interest in making the website available and keeping it secure.
  • Legal obligations and complaints. We use relevant information to meet our legal obligations, including keeping required records and handling data protection complaints. Where we need to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, we rely on our legitimate interest in protecting our legal rights.

Who receives your information

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Our email, telephone and messaging providers handle communications sent through their services. If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, WhatsApp also handles information under its own privacy notice. We may share relevant information with professional advisers, courts or public authorities where needed to deal with a legal matter or comply with the law.

See the GitHub privacy statement and the WhatsApp privacy policy for details of their handling of personal information.

International transfers

Our hosting and communications providers may process information outside the UK. Where we make a restricted transfer, we must use a mechanism permitted under UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards. You can contact us for details of the safeguards that apply and how to obtain a copy.

How long we keep information

Daily Shot does not keep a record of your visit to this website. GitHub manages the retention of its own security logs.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide while dealing with your enquiry, booking or complaint. We do not keep it afterwards unless the law requires us to retain specific records.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can ask us for access to your personal information, ask us to correct or erase it, ask us to restrict its use, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or ask for a portable copy of information you provided where the legal conditions apply. To exercise your rights, email or write to us using the contact details above.

How to complain

If you have a complaint about how we use your personal information, email us about your complaint or write to the address above. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, keep you informed and tell you the outcome without undue delay. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

For information about storage on your device and external links, see our cookie policy.
